04 / The DAA layer

Not just the nearest. The smartest.

ChargePath uses a greedy strategy: filter what is impossible, then take the best available score right now.

01

Filter

Remove unavailable stations and any stop outside your safe battery + distance window.

02

Score

Normalize distance and charging time, then blend them into one comparable match score.

03

Choose

Greedily select the highest score, with distance as the tie-breaker.

Current scoring formulamatch = 100 − (distance × 2.1) − (chargeTime × 0.55) − time overshoot + availability bonus
Battery feasibility is a hard gate, not a soft preference. That is why a farther available station can still beat a closer unavailable one.
